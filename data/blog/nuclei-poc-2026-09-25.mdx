### Ray &lt; 2.52.0 - Remote Code Execution

- **漏洞ID**: `CVE-2025-62593`
- **CVE**: `CVE-2025-62593` (2025)
- **严重程度**: 🔴 CRITICAL
- **风险等级**: 极高风险 (5/5)
